Project Name: End-to-End Communication System Simulator



Short Description:

This is a Python simulator that covers the full range of analog and digital communication systems. It takes you from signal generation, through modulation, noise modeling, and demodulation, all the way to filtering, FFT analysis, measuring SNR, using BPSK modulation, and analyzing BER.



Why This Project Matters:

The simulator gives hands-on experience with the core concepts used in RF engineering, digital signal processing, wireless systems, aerospace and defense work, and communication hardware jobs.



Main Technical Skills Used:

\- Python

\- NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib

\- Streamlit for dashboards

\- Signal generation

\- AM and DSB-SC modulation

\- Adding AWGN noise

\- Demodulating signals

\- Low-pass filtering

\- FFT analysis

\- BPSK modulation and BER measurement



Best Results:

You can use the simulator to see:

\- How baseband signals are created

\- AM and DSB-SC modulation in action

\- What happens as noise hits the channel

\- Recovering analog messages after transmission

\- Where carriers and sidebands sit in the spectrum via FFT

\- BPSK showing distinct phase jumps

\- BER getting better as Eb/N0 climbs

\- Adjust things live in the dashboard with Streamlit controls



What’s Next for Improvement:

The next steps? Adding QPSK and QAM modulation, constellation and eye diagrams, raised-cosine filters, plotting theoretical BER curves for comparison, and simulating carrier phase/frequency offsets. That’ll make it cover even more of what you’d see in real communication systems.
